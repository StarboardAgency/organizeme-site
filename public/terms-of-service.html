Terms of Service

Organize Me! Terms of Service · Published January 1, 2025

Initial Consultation Policy

We prioritize delivering personalized and efficient service to our clients from the very beginning of our engagement process. To facilitate this, we conduct all initial consultations exclusively through popular video conferencing platforms such as FaceTime and Zoom. This approach not only ensures a tailored experience but also enables potential clients to provide a comprehensive visual overview of their space using their camera, allowing us to better understand their unique organizational needs.

Safety Policy

To uphold the highest standards of safety and professionalism, we kindly request the presence of an adult female at the premises throughout the duration of our service engagements. This precautionary measure ensures a secure and comfortable environment for all parties involved, allowing us to deliver our services with utmost care and attention to detail.

Proposal Approval Policy

Upon completion of the initial consultation, we diligently prepare a detailed written proposal outlining the scope of work and associated costs. In adherence to our commitment to professionalism, we require that clients review the proposal thoroughly and provide their approval through a handwritten signature before any work commences. This formal acknowledgment ensures mutual understanding and agreement between both parties, facilitating a smooth and successful project execution.

Payment Policy

  • We require a 50% deposit of the total project cost before any work begins, with the remaining 50% due upon completion.
  • Payment methods accepted include Venmo and debit cards only, ensuring a secure and convenient transaction process for our clients.

Cancellation Policy

At Organize Me!, we understand that life can sometimes present unexpected changes and challenges. In order to best utilize our time and serve all of our clients effectively, we kindly request that any cancellations or rescheduling of appointments be made with a minimum of 48 hours notice. We value both your time and ours, and late cancellations or rescheduling may result in a fee of $100 to compensate for the reserved time slot. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in helping us maintain a productive and efficient schedule for all our clients.

Confidentiality Policy

We respect the privacy of our clients and the confidentiality of their spaces. Any sensitive information or personal items encountered during our service will be treated with utmost discretion.

Feedback Policy

We greatly value the feedback of our clients as it plays a crucial role in our continuous efforts to enhance and refine our services. We encourage clients to share their honest feedback upon completion of the project by completing our provided survey. Rest assured, all responses will be treated with the utmost confidentiality, ensuring that your input contributes directly to the ongoing improvement of our services. Thank you for helping us serve you better!

Sustainability Policy

We are committed to environmentally friendly practices and strive to minimize waste by recycling and responsibly disposing of any materials during our organization process.

Liability Policies

At Organize Me!, we prioritize the safety and well-being of our clients and team members. While we strive to deliver exceptional service in every aspect of our work, it's essential to outline our liability policy to ensure transparency and clarity.

Property Condition Assessment

Before commencing any organizational work, our team will conduct a thorough assessment of the client's property to identify any existing conditions that may pose a risk to our team members or the client.

Safety Measures

We adhere to strict safety protocols during our service, including wearing appropriate personal protective equipment and using safe practices when handling items or navigating through spaces.

Client Cooperation

Clients are expected to inform us of any hazardous conditions or safety concerns within their property before the commencement of our service. This includes but is not limited to, the presence of sharp objects, fragile items, or structural instabilities.

Limited Liability

While we take every precaution to ensure the safety of our team members and clients, Organize Me! shall not be held liable for any damages, injuries, or accidents that occur on the client's property during our service, unless such incidents are a direct result of negligence on our part.

Assumption of Risk

Clients acknowledge that engaging our services involves inherent risks associated with handling items, moving furniture, and navigating through spaces. By hiring Organize Me!, clients accept these risks and release us from any liability for accidents or damages that may occur during the course of our service.

Insurance Coverage

Organize Me! maintains comprehensive liability insurance coverage to protect both our clients and our business in the event of unforeseen circumstances or incidents. However, clients are encouraged to maintain their own homeowner's or renter's insurance policies for additional protection.

Claims Procedure

In the event of any damages or injuries occurring during our service, clients must notify us immediately. We will work diligently to address and resolve any issues through our insurance coverage and cooperate fully with the client to rectify the situation to the best of our ability.

Indemnification

Clients agree to indemnify and hold Organize Me!, its employees, contractors, and affiliates harmless from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising out of or related to our service, except to the extent caused by our gross negligence or willful misconduct.
